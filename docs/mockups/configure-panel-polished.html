Configure panel: three families, same system

The panel opens from a card's ⋯ menu and from "Add card". It lists the family's own fields, grouped as the descriptor says: the primary fields first, everything else folded under "More options". A field appears only when its condition holds (for example the Compare fields). Nothing runs until Apply, which stays disabled until the engine says the card is valid. Labels, choices and conditions are taken from configure_families.json; the question and cut names are the ones in the test fixtures.
