Benchmark tabs and narrow cards

The three benchmark tabs not drawn before, and the ranking, benchmark and relationship cards at narrow width. The narrow rule: a card's tabs always become a chip, because tab labels crowd a small card. Tables keep every column and scroll sideways with the first column pinned. Numbers are from the benchmark and relationship test fixtures (Uttar Pradesh, NDA focal party, round 2 against 2022 VS).

1. Comparison

Summary merges vote share and seats to read quickly. Comparison is the same numbers laid out in full: each measure with its round, its reference and the change, so a figure can be quoted or checked. The group headers span their columns.
