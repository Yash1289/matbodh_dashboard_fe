Grid card: two ways to lay out the controls

Same data as the Uttar Pradesh compare fixture (anchor 2027 VS CAPI R2, delta vs R1). Each option is shown at wide, medium and narrow widths, so the canvas can resize a card without redesigning it. Mock-up only, nothing here is built.

Option A: refined chips (keeps today's structure)

Cut selector stays on the left. The two controls you change most (Lens, Format) are visible. The rest go behind one "More" menu. A chip that differs from its default turns blue, so you can see what has been changed. Heat is a soft tint behind the value, not a saturated fill.

Option B: lens tabs plus a small tool row

The lens (what the numbers mean) becomes tabs, because it changes the whole reading of the table. The cut selector and the rest become small icon buttons with tooltips. Fewer words on screen, but the icons need learning.
